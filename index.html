About Us

A Church Rooted in Faith & Community

Welcome to AEBR Cyivugiza Church, a vibrant rural church located in Nyabirasi Sector, Rutsiro District, Western Province of Rwanda. We are committed to preaching the Gospel of Jesus Christ and transforming lives through faith, love, and community service. Our mission goes beyond spiritual growth—we actively promote healthy living and economic development among the people we serve.

Our Pastor

Since 2013

REV. Pastor Turimumahoro Etienne

Reveland Pastor

Leading Since:

I am Rev. Pastor TURIMUMAHORO Etienne, the Senior Pastor of Cyivugiza Local Baptist Church. I am called to lead people to God, helping them receive Jesus Christ as Lord and Savior and to live transformed lives through His love. My vision is to integrate the Gospel with community development by nurturing spiritual growth while also promoting improved living standards and economic empowerment. I believe the Good News should impact every area of life—heart, family, and daily livelihood.
