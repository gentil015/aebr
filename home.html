About Us

A Church Rooted in Faith & Community

Welcome to AEBR Cyivugiza Church, a vibrant rural church located in Nyabirasi Sector, Rutsiro District, Western Province of Rwanda. We are committed to preaching the Gospel of Jesus Christ and transforming lives through faith, love, and community service. Our mission goes beyond spiritual growth—we actively promote healthy living and economic development among the people we serve.
